# Sidralune manual

Welcome to the Sidralune manual. Sidralune is a free, offline-first astronomy suite for Windows that covers planning, telescope control, deep-sky and planetary imaging, image processing, and amateur science such as photometry, spectroscopy, astrometry and radio astronomy.

If you are new, start with [Getting started](Getting-started), then read [The user interface](The-user-interface). After that, jump to the workspace you need.

## Conventions in this manual

- Names of workspaces, tabs, buttons and fields are in **bold**, exactly as they appear in the program. An arrow shows navigation: **Settings → Data** means the **Data** tab of the **Settings** workspace.
- Coordinates are J2000 (ICRS) unless the text says "apparent" (of date, including precession, nutation and aberration).
- Times are shown in your site's local time zone in tables and charts, and in UTC where a file format or service requires it.

> Never point a telescope, finder or camera at the Sun without a certified solar filter covering the front of the instrument.
